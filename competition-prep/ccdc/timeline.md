## Invitational Details

- **Format:** virtual, 4-hour sessions (AM or PM slot)
- **Scoring:** results do NOT affect qualification — unscored dry run
- **Platform:** same NISE system used in real competition
- **Red team:** live professional red team throughout
- **Purpose:** full-speed dress rehearsal before Feb 13 Qualifier

**Why we skip Nov 14:**
Invitational 3 falls on the same day as the CyberForce Competition
(Nov 13–14). UIC cannot attend both. Oct 24 and Nov 7 give two full
practice runs before the Qualifier.

## Qualifier Details

**IL/MO State Qualifier — Feb 13, 2027**

- First scored round — results determine advancement
- 1st place in IL/MO → advances directly to Regionals (Mar 19–20)
- 2nd and 3rd place → advance to Wildcard (Feb 20)
- Below 3rd → season ends

**Wildcard — Feb 20, 2027**

- 2nd and 3rd place from every state qualifier compete
- Top finishers advance to Regionals
- This is the fallback path if the Qualifier does not go as planned

**Note on record participation:**
Over 40 Midwest teams anticipated for 2027 — a record number. Qualifier
field will be stronger than prior years. Invitational practice is not
optional.
